Set up in three steps

  1. Describe your brand

    Your name, what you sell and your store format. That sets who counts as a competitor and which places pull customers in.

  2. Say what matters to a new site

    Rate five things from not important to critical. Branchwise turns your answers into the weights behind every score.

  3. Get your ranked sites

    Sites come back ranked and at least 900 m apart, each with its reasons and risks. Save the good ones to a shortlist.

Five signals in every Branchwise score

Each zone is measured against the Foursquare places around it, then weighted by the priorities you set.

  • Competitors within 500 m, and your own stores nearby
  • Unis, malls, offices and stations within walking distance
  • Shops and venues within 300 m, a stand-in for foot traffic

Where does Branchwise get its place data?

Foursquare Open Source Places: about 233,000 open places in Greater Sydney, each tagged with a category.

Which parts of Sydney are covered?

Greater Sydney, from Parramatta to the coast and from the Northern Beaches to Hurstville. Other cities are not covered yet.

Does it find shops for lease?

Not yet. It tells you which zones suit your brand; you find the shopfront through your agent or a listings site. One store only? Skip the stores step.

Does Branchwise count real foot traffic?

No. It counts shops and venues within 300 m as a stand-in. It has no sensor, phone or sales data.

Who can see our stores and shortlist?

Only your account. Delete the account in Settings and your profile, stores and shortlist go with it.
