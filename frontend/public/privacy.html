Branchwise

Privacy

What Branchwise keeps about you, and how to remove it.

What we store

Your email address, a salted Argon2 hash of your password (never the password itself), your brand profile, the stores you add and your shortlist. Sessions are random tokens; we keep only a hash of each.

What we don’t do

No advertising trackers, no selling or sharing your data, and no access for other accounts. Your stores and shortlist are visible only to you.

Deleting your data

Delete your account in Settings. Your profile, stores, shortlist and sessions are removed at once.

Place data

Scores use Foursquare Open Source Places. Maps use OpenStreetMap data served by CARTO.
